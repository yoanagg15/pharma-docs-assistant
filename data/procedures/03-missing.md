## Count reconciliation
For missing items or packages, compare the receiving count against the delivery note and record both counts. Record the missing item identifiers when available and the delivery identifier. Ask the fictional distribution center to investigate the picking and dispatch records. Do not promise replacement stock before the investigation is reviewed.

## Complaint details
Record a missing-items complaint with a factual description, the affected package count, and a request for investigation or replacement review. An affected package count means packages with missing contents or absent packages recorded on the delivery note.
