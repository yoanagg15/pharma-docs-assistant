## Discrepancy record
For a delivery discrepancy, compare the delivery identifier, item labels, and delivery note. Describe the expected and observed details without modifying the original receiving record. Keep the affected packages separate pending review by the fictional distribution center. Do not infer that a different item is an acceptable substitute.

## Escalation and recording
Escalate unresolved delivery discrepancies to the fictional quality coordinator with the delivery identifier and complaint reference. The complaint record must contain the category, factual description, affected package count, and requested resolution. No real organization is contacted by this demonstration.
