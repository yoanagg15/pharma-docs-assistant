## Receiving damaged packages
For a delivery with damaged outer packaging, separate the affected packages from routine receiving stock and record the visible damage before further handling. Record the delivery identifier, affected package count, and a factual damage description. If safe, retain photographs of the outer packaging for the complaint record. Do not decide product suitability from packaging appearance alone; request a review by the fictional quality coordinator.

## Recording a complaint
Prepare a damaged-packaging complaint with the delivery identifier, damage description, affected package count, and requested resolution. Review the draft before explicitly confirming submission. A complaint reference acknowledges recording and does not authorize a replacement or return.
